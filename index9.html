Sundown GP

Slide your left thumb along the steer bar; the further from the middle, the harder it turns. Gas and brake are under your right thumb, with the handbrake above them. Rev selects reverse, and holding the brake at a standstill also backs up. Pause, camera and reset sit at the top of the screen. Turning the phone sideways gives a wider view.

Drive with W A S D or the arrow keys. Space is the handbrake and Shift fires the nitrous. In Online race, press N to start a race for everyone there. In the two realism modes, stop and press G to repair and refuel. X selects reverse and X again goes back to drive; with an automatic, holding the brake at a standstill also backs up. With a manual gearbox, E shifts up and Q shifts down. C cycles four camera views, hold V to look behind you, Tab or a click on the map expands it, L switches the headlights, Q and E are the left and right turn signals (in a manual car Q and E change gear, so the signals are on , and .), with an electric motor press 8 to fly and 8 again to land (Space climbs, Shift descends), H sounds the horn, R puts the car back on its wheels, P pauses and M mutes. In the stunt park, B jumps you straight to the boost strip.
